# LOC-05 private doctor specialty matching and schedule governance

The product PDF reserves the next 30 days for actual provider-confirmed working dates. The planned discovery and slot routes remain closed. This ticket provides private synthetic engineering support without adding patient traffic, public profiles, booking, OTP, clinical symptom-to-specialty inference or provider integrations.

## Operator workflow

1. Construct a strict version-1 `DoctorRevision` from authentic evidence collected outside the application. The record binds the PMDC registry identifier, professional name and approved qualifications, specialty taxonomy version and digest, exact LOC-04 hospital UUID and publication revision, and a schedule. Three bounded evidence references and SHA-256 digests represent professional registration, affiliation and schedule. The workflow verifies evidence bytes without retaining the underlying documents.
2. Provision separate non-inheriting, hospital-scoped PostgreSQL logins and backend allowlists for the importer, credential reviewer, schedule reviewer and operator. The source and reviewer registries start empty. Review keys, authorized facilities, source organizations and specialty scopes are provisioned independently. No role is inferred from hospital administration or a registration-shaped string.
3. The importer submits a draft. Each reviewer inspects that revision and independently verifies their source material. Signatures use distinct credential and schedule domains. Credential review binds the professional and affiliation documents, and schedule review binds the operating-hours evidence. Both proofs bind the exact draft digest. Rejected and correction-required decisions are recorded but cannot publish.
4. The operator rechecks both proofs and the taxonomy, then atomically activates the approved hospital-bound revision. Imports, reviews, events and revocations remain in private append-only history. Withdrawal and source/key revocation remove the active pointer. Review is required again for any revised affiliation, profile, schedule or exception.

Assignments are canonical specialty identifiers. A separately provisioned versioned vocabulary holds reviewed labels and exact aliases. Ambiguous aliases are rejected. Its exact digest is included in the reviewed doctor revision, and expiry or removal makes discovery unavailable. An alias is a lookup aid and does not infer medical equivalence. One practitioner has one supported registration and hospital relationship. Transferring a record to a different hospital is deferred pending a design that preserves historical appointment foreign keys.

## Schedule semantics

Schedules use Asia/Karachi local wall time. They bind weekdays, inclusive effective dates, same-day nonoverlapping sessions and whole slots. Overnight sessions and ambiguous overlapping exceptions are rejected. Leave, holidays, closure, cancellation and suspension suppress recurring hours. A one-day replacement requires affirmative reviewed sessions. A new schedule or exception requires a signed replacement revision. Current provider and hospital eligibility, taxonomy provenance and combined review expiry gate every read.

Private slot reads use an aware clock, a 0-to-30-day local horizon and a configurable lead time with a 15-minute engineering default. They emit UTC and local boundaries for complete unoccupied slots only. A narrow database routine checks interval overlap against pending and confirmed appointments and exposes a Boolean only. Canceled, rescheduled, completed and no-show rows do not hold a slot under the current active-start constraint. The existing foreign key requires the doctor and hospital to match. Read results are provisional, and a concurrent appointment can make an earlier snapshot stale. BK-series needs fresh identity and schedule checks, an atomic overlap-safe reservation, idempotency, OTP and confirmed delivery.

## Migration and acceptance

Migration `0010_doctor_governance` adds independent forced-RLS custody and projection roles, scoped operator assignments, lookup and active-occupancy indexes, and a narrow occupancy routine. Maintenance loses direct doctor and schedule mutations. Existing IDs, provider rows, appointment foreign keys and LOC-04 custody are preserved. Legacy flag-only rows are excluded from the new private discovery and slot services. Runtime details collapse unknown and ineligible doctors to avoid exposing private candidates. Query plans and single synthetic timings are captured in the artifact, while representative production performance remains unverified.

Rollback on an empty custody schema removes only the new roles, indexes, routines and history tables. A guarded downgrade refuses to discard custody records or scope assignments. Real data requires an audited backup, retention approval and a separate recovery procedure. The exact-head LOC-05 artifact verifies named cases, migration and prior location evidence. Authentic registration, affiliations, operating schedules, credential onboarding, privacy approvals and public release remain separate operational work.
