# GD-03 deterministic medication safety

GD-03 is a private, status-only check. It re-runs GD-01 for a typed server-owned assessment and current conversation, preserving emergency, urgent and unresolved-risk denial before considering a separately signed medication policy. GD-02 non-medication content and its review references provide no medication authority. The BE-05 public-facing default denial remains intact. No route, release flag, database grant or persistence changes.

MedicationEvidence records status, explicit answer, current/superseded provenance and source event for every age, pregnancy, allergy, condition and current medicine field. Only explicitly complete patient reports or clinical-source verification qualify, subject to the signed policy's stricter verified-field requirements. Empty lists can only qualify with complete provenance. A source event must belong to current authoritative context. Evidence digest changes, including corrections, invalidate the old authorization. Clinical verification labels must originate from a trusted evidence custodian. Production evidence issuance and atomic custody are unavailable. Verified applicability is required for a pregnancy not-applicable value.

A separately scoped, time-limited and revocable medication-policy key signs the reviewed policy/version, condition, product, vocabulary, ingredient mapping, exact signed formulary digest, exact GD-01 approval digest and current medication evidence digest. Policy/version must also be active in explicitly configured trusted composition. The GD-01 approval binds assessment, owner, revision, event and safety snapshot. Both registries are empty by default. No production policy issuance, normalization or reviewer operation is implemented.

The service verifies an exact active BE-05 candidate using its original domain and reviewer restrictions, with explicit reviewer revocation support. It then checks reviewed exact allergy/condition blocks and interactions through the shared BE-05 verified screening helper. Unknown codes or missing ingredient mappings fail closed. Ingredient intersection prevents duplicate exposure. No substring, brand guessing, unit conversion or pediatric interpolation. Exact product/version and formulation are bound through the signed record digest. The signed medication policy selects one exact candidate, with no automatic search across alternatives. A dose object is inspected only for type, and never returned or serialized by GD-03.

The engineering success status is synthetic_eligible and conveys no approved output. Tests use ephemeral keys and synthetic formulary data. The dedicated GD-03 suite isolates the guidance dependency with doubles; the existing GD-01 suite verifies its signed rule and content chain. Benchmark measurements cover aggregate status-only evaluation and default denial, excluding database, network and inference.

Clinical review still needs approved exact-product identity and ingredient/interactions coverage (including cross-reactivity where required), reviewed pediatric applicability, governed normalization and provenance issuance, QA-01 evaluation, approved EN/UR/PS content, and GD-04 publication, revocation and key custody. GD-02 durable history and atomic publisher controls remain open. Patient identity, privacy, consent, retention and authoritative version custody must be approved before real intake. No clinical speech or medication delivery is connected.

GD-05 adds an actual signed in-memory service chain alongside the prior guidance-double unit matrix.
